# Detailed Methods for Filling Each Block

## 1. Market Sizing Methods

**Bottom-up (preferred):**
1. Define precise ICP count (use LinkedIn filters, census, industry directories, app download estimates, etc.).
2. Estimate realistic conversion or penetration in Year 1–3.
3. Multiply by expected ARPU / ACV.
4. SOM = what is reachable with current or planned distribution in 12–36 months.

**Top-down sanity check:**
- Industry reports (Gartner, Forrester, Statista, IBISWorld, public company filings).
- Always discount heavily for “addressable” vs total market.

**Why Now signals:**
- Search volume trends (Google Trends, Exploding Topics, Ahrefs/Semrush)
- Funding waves in related categories
- Regulatory changes
- Technology cost curves (AI inference, sensors, etc.)
- Behavioral shifts post major events

## 2. Pain Discovery Methods

**Primary (highest quality):**
- 20–30 Mom-Test style interviews
- Focus on past behavior: “Tell me about the last time…”

**Secondary (research phase):**
- Mine Reddit, Quora, Indie Hackers, Twitter/X, Facebook groups, Discord
- G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot 1–2 star reviews of existing solutions
- Support ticket themes or public complaint data
- Job postings that reveal internal pain (e.g. “looking for someone to manually do X”)

**Scoring:**
- Frequency (daily / weekly / monthly / rare)
- Intensity (annoying → painful → critical)
- Current cost (hours + money + emotional + risk)

## 3. Solution & MVP Scope

- Start from the primary Job-to-be-Done.
- List all possible features, then ruthlessly cut to Must-have only.
- Ask: “If this feature did not exist, would the core outcome still be delivered for the ICP?”
- Prefer low-fidelity tests: landing page, Concierge, spreadsheet MVP, no-code prototype.

## 4. Edge & Competition

**Competitive Matrix columns (example):**
- Target ICP
- Core value prop
- Pricing model
- Key strengths
- Key weaknesses / complaints
- Distribution strength
- Estimated traction

**Moat evaluation (Hamilton Helmer 7 Powers):**
- Scale Economies
- Network Effects
- Counter-Positioning
- Switching Costs
- Branding
- Cornered Resource
- Process Power

Positioning formula:
“For [ICP] who [struggle with pain], [Product] is a [category] that [key outcome]. Unlike [alternative], we [unique difference].”

## 5. Customer Definition

**ICP filters (make them conjunctive):**
- Firmographic / demographic
- Behavioral (tools they already use, budget authority)
- Trigger events
- Psychographic (values, sophistication)

**Distribution ranking criteria:**
- Speed of feedback loop
- Expected CAC
- Ability to reach exact ICP
- Scalability later

## 6. Value & Economics

**Willingness-to-Pay evidence hierarchy:**
1. They already pay for imperfect solutions
2. Pre-orders / deposits / LOIs
3. Strong language in interviews (“I would pay $X tomorrow”)
4. Van Westendorp or Gabor-Granger surveys

**Unit Economics formulas:**
- LTV ≈ ARPU × Gross Margin % × (1 / monthly churn)
- CAC = (Sales + Marketing spend) / New customers
- Target: LTV:CAC ≥ 3, Payback ≤ 12 months

**Pricing approaches:**
- Value-based (share of value created)
- Competitive (anchor to current alternatives)
- Cost-plus (rarely primary for software)

## Validation Scorecard Guidance

Score each block 1–10:
- 1–3: Weak or contradictory evidence
- 4–6: Some signal but major gaps
- 7–8: Solid secondary research + some primary
- 9–10: Strong primary evidence + clear economics

Overall recommendation thresholds:
- ≥ 7.5 and no critical block < 6 → Strong candidate
- 6–7.4 → Promising but needs specific experiments
- < 6 → High risk, do not build large yet
